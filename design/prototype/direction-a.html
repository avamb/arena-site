How it works

On sale today. Three questions organizers ask first.

  1. How fast can I go on sale?

    An event without a seating plan is on sale within the hour: name, dates, prices, your poster, your payment account, done. A seated venue takes a seating plan, which we draw with you from your plan or SVG, usually within a day.

  2. What do I need?

    The event name and schedule, your ticket prices, your poster, and your own payment account: Stripe, Flitt, AllPay or another provider of your country. Money goes to you from the first ticket.

  3. Which way do I set it up?

    In the browser, in the event center. Or in Telegram, where the bot reads your poster and fills in dates, prices and the venue for you to confirm. Or send it to us and we set it up.

What organizers ask next

Yes. And here is how.

Can I use promo codes?

Yes. A code can apply to one event, one session or one category, with a window, a minimum and a per-buyer limit.

Can prices change on a date I set?

Yes. Early-bird windows switch by date and time. Quantity steps open the next price when the current one sells out.

Several prices for one event?

Yes. Categories with their own price, quota and sale window, plus free and pay-what-you-want tickets.

Can I add a service fee?

Yes. A percentage per sales channel, shown to the buyer as a separate line and paid into your account with the ticket.

With seats and without?

Yes, and both in one event: a seating plan with categories, standing areas with quotas. Send us your venue plan as a PDF, a spreadsheet or a hand sketch: we build it into your event. You never draw it yourself.

Does a scanner work with the tickets?

Yes. Every ticket is a PDF with an EAN-13 barcode and a QR, in the buyer's language. Scan at the door with the scanner app for iOS and Android or a handheld scanner. The app also takes tickets sold by your partners in other systems.

Money

Buyers pay you. Not us.

100% of every sale goes to you. The buyer pays the ticket price, and the service fee if you add one, straight into your own payment account, in your currency, on your statement. Arena never holds your money. After each session you pay Arena for the platform service, per event. Refunds and chargebacks are your decision, not a platform policy.
