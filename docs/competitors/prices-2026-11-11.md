# Competitor prices behind the "alternative" pages (checked 11.11.2026)

Source for every number on `/eventbrite-alternative`, `/ticketspice-alternative`, `/ticketsource-alternative`
and `/weezevent-alternative`. Ticket Tailor numbers are in the comment of `content/en/pages/tickettailor-alternative.yaml`.
Re-check every quarter and before changing a page: open each URL, compare, update the page in all 9 languages.
Only rows we could read on an official page go into a table. Anything else stays off the pages.

## Eventbrite
- US: service fee 3.7% + US$1.79 per paid ticket; payment processing 2.9% of the order, separate.
- UK: 6.95% + £0.59 per paid ticket; payment processing "not applicable" (not charged separately).
- Ireland: 5.9% + €0.79 per paid ticket; payment processing "not applicable".
  Source: https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/755615/how-much-does-it-cost-for-organizers-to-use-eventbrite/ (read 11.11.2026)
- Free tickets: no fee. Buyer pays by default, organizer can absorb. Source: https://www.eventbrite.com/organizer/pricing/
- Payouts: Eventbrite collects the money and pays out after the event (3 days after it ends in the help centre,
  "no later than 5 business days" in the merchant agreement), pre-event payouts on conditions.
  Source: https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/426093/
- Has a marketplace (event discovery). We do not.
- Other euro countries are "legacy packages" with different rates: not used on the page.

## TicketSpice (Webconnex)
- Per paid ticket: Standard US$0.99, Premium US$1.49, Pro US$1.99. No subscription, no setup fee.
- Reserved seating: Premium and Pro only, not Standard. The organizer builds the chart in a venue builder.
- Card processing: 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction, separate.
  Source: https://www.ticketspice.com/pricing (read 11.11.2026)
- Payouts: weekly on Wednesdays through Webconnex Payments, after identity verification; daily only on Pro by approval.
  Source: https://help.ticketspice.com/en/articles/14172125
- Webconnex Payments: USD and CAD only.

## TicketSource
Read in the browser on 11.11.2026 (automated fetchers get HTTP 403; ticketsource.co.uk sits behind a Cloudflare check,
the same prices are on ticketsource.com).
- UK, https://www.ticketsource.com/pricing : TicketSource processing 7% + VAT per paid booking, minimum £0.25 + VAT,
  card processing included; own Stripe account 4.5% + VAT per paid booking.
- EU, https://www.ticketsource.com/es/pricing : TicketSource processing 8.5% per paid booking, minimum €0.30,
  card processing included; own Stripe account 4.5% per paid booking. No VAT mentioned.
- Both: free events and in-person bookings free; no contracts or tiers; seating plans "full access".
- Payouts with their processing: the Monday after the event. With own Stripe: directly, with every payment.
- Registered charities: a discount on both options, amount not published.

## Weezevent (France)
- Online ticketing in EUR: 2.5% per ticket sold online, minimum €0.99 incl. VAT, bank fees included. No subscription.
- Free events: free.
- Payouts: every 15 days ("Recettes versées tous les 15 jours").
- Numbered seating (2D or 3D): only in the tailor-made offer, price on request.
  Source: https://weezevent.com/fr/nos-tarifs/ and https://weezevent.com/en-gb/our-prices/ (read 11.11.2026)
- Also sells cashless/RFID, staff accreditation, on-site POS. We do not offer these.

## Honesty notes
- Weezevent and TicketSource (default option) include card processing in their fee. Our commission does not:
  the buyer's card fee is charged by the organizer's own payment provider. Pages say so plainly and do not claim
  a lower total against Weezevent.
- Eventbrite and TicketSpice charge processing separately, like us, so the commission rows compare like for like.

## Exchange rate used on the pages
- ECB reference rate of 11.11.2026: 1 EUR = 1.1127 USD = 0.83618 GBP, so US$1 = £0.7515.
  Source: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html
- Only TicketSource needs it: our commission outside the EU is in US dollars, their prices are in pounds.
  Euro comparisons (Weezevent, Eventbrite Ireland) use our own euro price, no conversion.
